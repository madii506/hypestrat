01 — The strategy

Companies stack BTC. Coins should stack HYPE.

Treasury companies turned a balance sheet into a trade. HYPESTRAT does it for memecoins: a coin's creator fees buy HYPE, the HYPE gets staked, and the treasury sits at a public Hyperliquid address anyone can check.

    06 — FAQ

    Questions.

    What is HYPESTRAT?

    A HYPE treasury in every coin. A coin's creator fees buy HYPE on Hyperliquid and stack it in that coin's own treasury, at a public address.

    Where do the creator fees go?

    Into HYPE. Fees are claimed in SOL, moved to Hyperliquid, swapped into HYPE on the spot market and staked with a validator.

    How do I know a treasury is real?

    Paste its address into Check a treasury. Spot HYPE, staked HYPE and every HYPE trade are read live from Hyperliquid. You can also open the address on Hypurrscan.

    What do HYPE per 1M and mNAV mean?

    HYPE per 1M is how much HYPE backs every million tokens (pump.fun coins have a 1B supply). mNAV is market cap divided by the treasury's value. Below 1, the coin trades for less than its HYPE.

    When can I launch my own?

    Launches open after $HYPESTRAT goes live. Build your strategy card now and post it.

    Is HYPESTRAT part of pump.fun or Hyperliquid?

    No. It's independent and built on both.

    Is any of this a promise of returns?

    No. A treasury moves with the price of HYPE, and it can go down. Nothing here is financial advice.
